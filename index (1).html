Our Story

Tavi's M Nail Spa was born from a passion for artistic beauty and holistic self-care. Our founder, Monicah, envisioned a sanctuary where meticulous nail artistry meets tranquil relaxation.

With over a decade in the beauty industry and training from Parisian nail academies, Monicah personally trains each technician to deliver exceptional service using only premium, nourishing products.
